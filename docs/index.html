The Rent Split Verdict

Equal splits are lazy, not fair. One room has the ensuite and the light. Another hears the street. This prices every room on what it actually is — common space split equally, private space weighted by size, bathroom, and perks — and ends the argument with math instead of volume.

The Lease

Kitchen, living room, halls — everyone uses them, everyone splits them equally. The rest is priced room by room. 25–35% is typical.

Method: fair rent = (common pool ÷ rooms) + room pool × (room weight ÷ total weight). Weight = sq ft × bathroom factor (ensuite 1.20 · private half 1.10 · shared 1.00) + perk equivalents in sq ft (parking +15, balcony +12, walk-in closet +8, great light +6, street noise −12, tiny closet −6). Not legal or tax advice — it is an argument settler, not a lease.
